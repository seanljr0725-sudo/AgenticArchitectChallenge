Fictional Employee Handbook — SAMPLE DATA ONLY
This handbook describes the invented company Cedar Example Sdn Bhd. It is demonstration data, not legal advice or a real employment policy.

Annual leave
Employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per year. Annual leave requests require manager approval at least five working days in advance.

Medical leave
Employees receive 14 days of paid medical leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than two consecutive working days.

Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with prior manager approval.

Professional training allowance
Employees receive RM1,200 per year for approved professional training. Unused training allowance does not carry forward to the next year.

Working hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday through Friday, including a one-hour lunch break.

Equipment
The company provides one laptop to each employee. Company equipment must be returned on the final working day.

Expense reimbursement
Approved business expenses must be submitted with receipts within 30 calendar days of purchase.
